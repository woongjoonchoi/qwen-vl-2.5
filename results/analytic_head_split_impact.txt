 Summary:
  • Head-split improves window-attn KERNEL by 14-41% (memory-BW reduction 37.5%)
  • But kernel is only 0.04-0.2% of VE → VE improvement: 0.03-0.67ms per resolution
  • TTFT improvement: 0.03-0.70ms (< 0.3%)
  • Output tok/s: UNCHANGED (bottleneck is LLM decode, not VE)
  • Root cause of small E2E gain: Amdahl — kernel is 0.04-0.2% of TTFT

  • Real leverage: QKV/MLP (75% of VE), not attention kernel (2% of VE)
  • Full-attn×4 blocks (22% of VE at 1792px): compute-bound, head-split irrelevant
